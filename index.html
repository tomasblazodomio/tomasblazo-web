About Tomas Blazo

Publishing and Writing Expert

I did not take the traditional path to becoming a writer.

My first book sold well enough that I decided to build something bigger. I founded a publishing house that brought authors like Arnold Schwarzenegger and Jordan Belfort to Central European readers. For 15 years I ran that company, worked with global names and published titles that sold hundreds of thousands of copies. Authors I worked with include New York Times bestsellers such as Mitch Albom, Bronnie Ware and Randy Gage.

Then it all fell apart.

Four years behind bars with nothing but a pen, notebook pages and time taught me more about the raw truth of storytelling than any boardroom ever could. I have seen the view from the top. I have lived at rock bottom. Between those two worlds I held onto the only constant that kept me sane: writing.

I read manuscripts for a living, so I know the exact page where a reader quits. I will not tell you your book is great when it isn't. Flattery is the most expensive thing a writer can buy.

On Substack I write:

  • The Other Side of the Desk: my main series, including The Writer's Blueprint, a framework on how to outline, write, finish and publish books that sell, built on 15 years of industry experience.
  • Behind the Purple Door: my true prison story.
  • Short stories and serialized novels.
